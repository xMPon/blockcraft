# Code of Conduct

BlockCraft should be a friendly, practical place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect maintainers' time by keeping issues and pull requests clear and scoped.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Repeated off-topic disruption
- Publishing private information without permission
- Bad-faith reports or attempts to abuse project infrastructure

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, close issues or pull requests,
or block contributors who violate this code of conduct. If you see a problem,
contact a maintainer privately where possible.
